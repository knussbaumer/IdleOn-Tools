Getting your save

  1. Open IdleonToolbox with your account loaded.
  2. Export your account data as a JSON file.
  3. In any tool, pick the file, drop it on the import box, or paste its content. Each tool remembers your last import in this browser.

Game data, formulas and the save parser come from IdleonToolbox by Morta1 (GPL-3.0), bundled into each tool. Game icons belong to Lavaflame2 / Legends of IdleOn.
